Sister on Both Sides

2026 · Afya Shaikh · View on Medium

Have you ever had someone who loves to spite you specifically?

Someone who seeks out your opinions only to ignore you and do the complete opposite.

A person who, when you were younger, you could not stand, and they couldn’t stand you, either.

But then something changes, and you start to see them differently.

Maybe you start to like them a little.

Someone who hums the song that has been stuck in your head.

Someone where you both think the same weird thought at the same time.

Then there are those days when you both agree to hang out but end up doing your things. Like, you are together but also apart, and somehow it works.

Someone who you create those fake YouTube videos with, acting like a little comedy duo, creating a memory you will remember when you scroll far back.

Those moments of awkward silence. You know, the ones that used to make you cringe and wrinkle your nose? Now that silence is quiet, peaceful, comfortable, even. A point where you both have accepted that sometimes, you don’t need to fill the loud silence. It’s like a calm lake where the stillness speaks more than words ever could.

They would push you off the diving board without warning, but obviously, you’re dragging them down with you.

But the thing about this person that really sets them apart is how well they understand you. It’s like they have a direct line to your thoughts and feelings in a way that friends never quite seem to get.

Now, whenever you look at their eyes,

the same ones you’ve played with,

the same ones you’ve argued with,

the same ones you’ve danced and cried with,

you see the same child you grew up with.

The same kid who used to drive you crazy is now an irreplaceable part of your life.

I have that person.

And even though we don’t share the same blood,

we are still sisters by heart.
